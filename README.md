<a id="overview"></a>

**HTTPS Everywhere** is a powerful Firefox plugin designed to enhance your browsing security by automatically directing you to the HTTPS version of websites whenever possible. This ensures your communications are encrypted, protecting your data from potential threats. With the **latest version** of HTTPS Everywhere, you can enjoy a comprehensive and secure browsing experience without any limitations.
